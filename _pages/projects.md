## German Research Foundation (DFG): The Politics of International Humanitarian News

<p align="center">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/img/ifkwdfg.jpg' | relative_url }}" 
       alt="VisFrameCluster overview" 
       width="350">
</p>

This project uses a decade-long corpus of over one million news articles, applying natural language processing and machine learning to examine the framing (paper 1), journalistic role performance (paper 2), and agenda-setting (paper 3) of international humanitarian news.

- **Duration:** 09/2024 – 03/2027
- **Grant Amount:** €221,400
- **PI:** Dr. Zhe Xu (LMU Munich, Germany)
- **Collaborators:** Prof. Dr. Martin Scott (University of East Anglia, UK); Prof. Dr. Dani Madrid-Morales (University of Sheffield, UK); Prof. Dr. Kate Wright (University of Edinburgh, UK); Prof. Dr. Yotam Ophir (University at Buffalo, US); Prof. Dr. Liane Rothenberger (Otto-Friedrich-Universität Bamberg, Germany)
- **Research Team:** Michela Cimarelli (LMU Munich); Shuai Wang (LMU Munich)
