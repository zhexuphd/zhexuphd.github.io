I am an early-career computational social scientist in the [Department of Media and Communication](https://www.ifkw.uni-muenchen.de/index.html) at [LMU Munich](https://www.lmu.de/en/), affiliated with the chairs of [Prof. Dr. Thomas Hanitzsch](https://www.ifkw.uni-muenchen.de/organisation/personen/professoren/hanitzsch_thomas/index.html) and [Prof. Dr. Neil Thurman](https://neilthurman.com/), where I serve as PI of a [German Research Foundation (DFG)](https://www.dfg.de/) [Walter Benjamin](https://www.dfg.de/en/research-funding/funding-opportunities/programmes/individual/walter-benjamin) project. I also serve as a [research fellow](https://ptml.sjmc.wisc.edu/staff/xu-zhe/) at the [Public Tech Media Lab](https://ptml.sjmc.wisc.edu/) at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://www.wisc.edu/).

I hold degrees in engineering science and media studies and received my PhD in 2023 from the [University of Cologne](https://www.uni-koeln.de/en/). While there, I served as an associate lecturer in media studies and received training in natural language processing and machine learning from the [Center for Data and Simulation Science](https://cds.uni-koeln.de/en/) and the [Department of Digital Humanities](https://dh.phil-fak.uni-koeln.de/). I was also a visiting scholar at the [London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE)](https://www.lse.ac.uk/).

I work in media ethics, computational journalism, and computational social science, answering questions about: (1) how information ecosystems shape public perceptions and decision-making around humanitarian issues; (2) how emerging technologies, including virtual reality, algorithms, and artificial intelligence, are transforming journalism and media institutions; and (3) how computational methods, including unsupervised clustering, semantic embeddings, and multimodal large language models, can be developed, validated, and theoretically grounded for communication research (see my open-source tools [bertopic4news](https://github.com/yuysky/bertopic4news) and VisFrameCluster). My work has appeared in *Journalism & Mass Communication Quarterly*, *International Communication Gazette*, and *International Journal of Communication*, among other peer-reviewed journals. The [DFG](https://www.dfg.de/), the [Alexander von Humboldt Foundation](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/), and [LMU Munich](https://www.lmu.de/en/) have supported my work.

For academic service, I review for journals including [Digital Journalism](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/rdij20), [New Media & Society](https://journals.sagepub.com/home/nms), [Social Science & Medicine](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/social-science-and-medicine), [Journalism](https://journals.sagepub.com/home/jou), and [Humanities and Social Sciences Communications](https://www.nature.com/palcomms/), and I serve as a grant and conference reviewer for the [Czech Science Foundation (GACR)](https://gacr.cz/en/), [ECREA](https://ecrea.eu/), and [IAMCR](https://iamcr.org/). I was also one of the core planners of the [8th International Conference on World-Class Universities](https://soe.sjtu.edu.cn/En/Show/781) at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/).
