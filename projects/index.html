The Politics of Humanitarian News (Funded by DFG)

This project uses natural language processing, large language models, and network analysis with a decade-long corpus of over one million news articles to examine journalistic practices across political and media systems in the context of humanitarian crises.

  • Duration: September 2024 – March 2027
  • Grant amount: €221,430
  • Principal Investigator: Dr. Zhe Xu, LMU Munich
